What we found / 1 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

Search is told to stay out.

Robots.txt blocks compliant crawlers across the site. Search engines may miss fresh reasons to visit. Some pages still appear in search; no deindexing or traffic loss proved. Crawl rule ↗

02

Food clicks can take a detour.

The first homepage button leads to slots. If a food ad leads here, guests must find dining again rather than going straight to a menu or booking. Website check ↗

03

Slow first homepage view.

October 20’s simulated slow-4G homepage test showed main content at 14.2 seconds. A difficult connection may lose a guest. Separate 28-day real-visit data for this page passed Google’s checks: 1.3 seconds. Google speed report ↗

What we found / 2 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

The homepage has no video.

No video player appeared in our homepage check. Guests cannot watch a short dining or arrival story there before choosing their evening. Videos elsewhere were not ruled out. Website check ↗

05

Search-data format is unused.

The homepage has no JSON-LD business data. Adding this common format would give search engines another clear record of venue details. Other pages were not checked; no rich-result guarantee. Website check ↗

What we found / 3 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

06

Page labels do not help.

The homepage has no main heading; image descriptions include “alt” and “Secondary Image”. Screen-reader users may struggle to follow the choices before planning a visit. Website check ↗

07

No homepage social route.

The homepage has no Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube links. Guests not ready to book have no direct route from this page to follow upcoming content. Website check ↗

How we would help / 1 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

01

Let search read the right pages

Confirm the crawl policy with your web team, allow intended public pages and check the sitemap and search reports after release.

02

Send each ad to its promise

Route dining and event ads to matching pages with menus, dates, arrival details and a clear next step.

03

Lighten the first view

Resize oversized media, reduce files that delay the first view and retest before adding video. Compare controlled tests with real-visit data.

How we would help / 2 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

04 + 07

Show the night, then keep in touch

Add a lightweight dining preview and clear links to verified social accounts, so guests can see the experience and follow what is next.

05

Explain the venue to search

Add accurate business data to the homepage, then check restaurant and event pages for appropriate details without promising special search displays.

06

Make the page easier to follow

Use a clear main heading and meaningful descriptions for informative images. Leave decorative images silent and test keyboard and screen-reader navigation.
