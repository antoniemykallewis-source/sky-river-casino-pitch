Finding 1 of 3

Check the search rule.

What we saw

Your robots.txt file tells search crawlers not to read the site.

Why it matters

Search may miss fresh restaurant details. This does not prove you are absent from Google.

Our fix

We’d ask if the rule is intended, then correct it and check access if needed.

Crawl rule ↗
Finding 2 of 3

Help screen readers explain the page.

What we saw

Homepage code has no main heading. Some image descriptions are vague or blank.

Why it matters

That can make the page harder to follow when read aloud.

Our fix

We’d add headings and review image words, leaving decorative ones blank. Then test keyboard and read-aloud tools.

Homepage ↗
Finding 3 of 3

Send diners straight to dining.

What we saw

The first homepage button leads to slots; dining appears later.

Why it matters

Food-ad visitors may need to find the restaurant again.

Our fix

We’d send each food or event ad to its matching page, with a short film and clear arrival or booking steps.
